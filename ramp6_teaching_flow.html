About This Guide

RAMP 6 is an accelerated course that covers 7th grade Florida BEST standards alongside selected 8th grade content. This guide sequences those standards to maximize conceptual coherence across both grade levels — building number sense foundations first, followed by algebraic reasoning, and culminating in Grade 8 extensions that bridge toward Algebra 1.

How to Use

  • Numbered cards show the recommended teaching order within each quarter — start at 1 and work down.
  • Teal-bordered cards indicate Grade 8 standards embedded in this accelerated course.
  • Color-coded strand pills (NSO, AR, GR, DP) identify the domain of each standard.
  • Quarter 4 is a spiral & enrichment quarter — clusters bridge toward 9th grade Algebra 1.
📌
Important — Recommendation, Not Mandate: The instructional sequence shown here is a recommendation based on conceptual coherence and pacing research. Teachers and curriculum coaches should adapt the order to fit their students’ needs, their school calendar, and district pacing guides. Professional judgment always comes first.

Recommended Teaching Sequence — RAMP 6 ADVANCED

RAMP 6 is an accelerated course that covers 7th grade standards alongside selected 8th grade content. Standards within each quarter are ordered to maximize conceptual coherence — number sense foundations first, algebraic applications next, and Grade 8 extensions last. Cross-quarter notes highlight where earlier skills unlock later content and where the course bridges toward 9th grade Algebra. Grade 8 standards are marked with a teal border.

★ QUARTER 4

TEST PREP & FAST PM3 REVIEW

All 31 RAMP 6 standards reviewed in six strategic clusters  ·  Spiraled practice toward Algebra I readiness

Quarter 4 is a strategic, spiraled review of all Q1, Q2, and Q3 standards in preparation for the FAST PM3 assessment. Standards are grouped into six clusters by conceptual strand. Start with Number Sense (Cluster 1) to re-activate rational number fluency that supports every other cluster, then move through algebra, proportional reasoning, geometry, linear functions, and data. Grade 8 standards are embedded throughout and should be reviewed in the context of the Grade 7 standards they extend.

CLUSTER 1 — NUMBER SENSE
Week 1
7.NSO.1.2Multiply & Divide Rationals
7.NSO.2.2All 4 Operations — Fluency
7.NSO.2.3Real-World Rational Problems
7.NSO.2.1Multi-Step Rational Problems
8.NSO.1.1Square & Cube Roots
8.NSO.1.7Rational & Irrational Application
Focus: Four operations with signed rational numbers; evaluating roots of perfect squares/cubes; approximating and ordering irrational values.
⚠ Common error: Sign errors with negative fractions; confusing √2 as rational because it looks "simple."
CLUSTER 2 — ALGEBRA & EQUATIONS
Weeks 2–3
7.AR.2.1Algebraic Expressions
7.AR.2.2Two-Step Equations
8.AR.2.2Systems — Graphically
8.AR.2.3Systems — Real-World Problems
Focus: Evaluating and simplifying expressions with rational coefficients; writing and solving equations; identifying and interpreting system solutions as intersection points.
⚠ Common error: Distributing incorrectly with negative coefficients; selecting the wrong intersection point on a system graph.
CLUSTER 3 — PROPORTIONAL & FINANCIAL
Week 3
7.AR.3.1Percent Problems
7.AR.3.2Simple & Compound Interest
7.AR.3.3Percent Change
7.AR.4.5Proportional vs. Non-Proportional
Focus: Tax, tip, discount, markup; applying I = Prt and compound interest formula; calculating percent change; distinguishing proportional and non-proportional relationships.
⚠ Common error: Confusing percent of a number with percent change; applying simple interest formula to compound interest scenarios.
CLUSTER 4 — GEOMETRY
Week 4
7.GR.1.1Angle Relationships
7.GR.1.3Triangle Angle Sum
7.GR.1.4Triangle Inequality
7.GR.1.5Polygon Angle Sums
7.GR.1.2Scale Drawings
7.GR.2.1Circumference & Area of Circles
7.GR.2.2Area of Composite Figures
7.GR.2.3Volume & Surface Area
Focus: Equation-writing for angle problems; applying (n−2)×180°; π-based area/circumference; decomposing composite figures; 3D surface area and volume.
⚠ Common error: Using triangle angle sum for quadrilaterals; forgetting to divide by 2 for semi-circles; confusing lateral vs. total surface area.
CLUSTER 5 — LINEAR FUNCTIONS
Weeks 5–6
7.AR.4.1Determine Slope
7.AR.4.2Graph & Write y = mx + b
7.AR.4.3Interpret Slope & y-Intercept
7.AR.4.4Compare Linear Relationships
8.AR.3.1Is It a Function?
8.AR.3.2Linear vs. Nonlinear
8.AR.3.4Compare Two Linear Functions
Focus: Calculating and interpreting slope from all representations; writing and graphing y = mx + b; classifying relations as functions; comparing two linear functions side-by-side.
⚠ Common error: Inverting rise/run; failing the vertical line test on curved graphs; confusing rate of change with initial value when comparing functions.
CLUSTER 6 — DATA ANALYSIS
Week 7
7.DP.1.3Statistical Displays
7.DP.1.4Select Measures & Interpret
Focus: Reading and constructing histograms, box plots, and dot plots; choosing mean vs. median for skewed data; identifying outliers and explaining their effect on center and spread.
⚠ Common error: Using the mean when a strong outlier is present; confusing IQR with range; misreading histogram bar heights as individual values.
🎯 FAST PM3 Strategy for RAMP 6: The PM3 assessment is cumulative across all 31 standards. Begin Cluster 1 (Number Sense) first — rational number fluency underpins every other cluster. Reserve the most class time for Cluster 5 (Linear Functions), as the 8th grade extensions (AR.3.1, 3.2, 3.4) are the most novel content and carry high conceptual demand. Use mixed-review problem sets between clusters to build retrieval strength across all six strands. Students completing RAMP 6 successfully are positioned to enter Algebra I with strong linear function intuition and equation-solving confidence.
